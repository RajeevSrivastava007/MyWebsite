A Short Introduction

I was born in Lucknow and grew up there. My working life started in the service industry on a non-technical process — handling customer queries, bookings and escalations — and that first year taught me what it means to be accountable to the person on the other end of the line. From there I moved into IT: service desk engineering, and then a command centre supporting global enterprise operations.

The years that followed took me through Gurugram, Noida and Delhi, and remotely alongside teams in Bengaluru and Dubai. Delhi NCR gave me far more than a career. It gave me experience I will carry for the rest of my life, and it is where I met my wife.

I owe a great deal to the organisations I worked with along the way. Each one trusted me with something larger than the last, and much of what I know today was built inside those teams. In December 2025 I lost my father. A few months later my role came to an end. I came home to Lucknow, took a long pause, and decided to build something of my own. That became Veeraj Consulting, named for my parents.

This is the personal half of my story, and I will keep adding to it — college, the move to Delhi, the food I love, and the things I do when I am not working.

A New Beginning

Career transitions are never easy, especially when they happen during an already difficult personal phase.

After losing my father in December 2025, I returned to work and soon found myself navigating an unexpected career transition. Like many others in the industry, I began looking for my next opportunity.

The job market was, and is, challenging. Thousands of applications for a single opening. Highly experienced professionals competing for roles below their previous levels. Interview after interview. There were moments of self-doubt, uncertainty and anxiety — especially while managing financial commitments and responsibilities.

But this period also gave me something I had rarely had before: time to pause and reflect.

I moved back to my home city, spent more time with family and friends, and took a step back to think about what I really wanted to do next. I evaluated my experience, my strengths, the problems I enjoy solving, and the kind of work I truly value.

Eventually I realised that instead of continuously looking for the next job, I could build something of my own around what I already know and enjoy. That led me to start Veeraj Consulting.

What initially felt like an uncertain career gap became a period of reflection, learning, family, and finally, a new beginning. I am grateful for the experiences that brought me here, and excited about what comes next.
